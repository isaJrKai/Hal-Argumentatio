# HAL Constitution v0.1

**Purpose**: Define the long-term vision, architecture, principles, and engineering direction for HAL as an AI Business Operating Intelligence System.

## 1. Mission
Help users make better business decisions through memory, reasoning, planning, execution, and learning.

## 2. Principles
* **Ready beats perfect**: Deliver tangible value continuously.
* **Preserve architecture**: Respect and extend existing modular patterns.
* **Modularity**: Build independent intelligence subsystems.
* **Evidence-based recommendations**: Base actions on live website, geo, and sentiment audits.
* **Continuous learning**: Calibrate system weights after campaign outcomes.

## 3. North Star
HAL is a Business Operating Intelligence Platform, not just a chatbot or CRM.

## 4. Architecture
`Mission` ➔ `Goals` ➔ `Planner` ➔ `Memory` ➔ `Knowledge` ➔ `World Model` ➔ `Agents` ➔ `Skills` ➔ `Tools` ➔ `Integrations` ➔ `Execution` ➔ `Learning`.

## 5. Core Skills
* Conversation & Reasoning
* Memory Isolation
* Knowledge Graph Construction
* Geo Intelligence & Territory Scouting
* Website Intelligence & SEO Audits
* Reputation & Sentiment Tracking
* Financial Analytics & Forecasting
* Document Engineering
* Workflow Automation

## 6. Agents
* **Planner**: Deconstructs targets into scheduled events.
* **Memory**: Isolates working, episodic, and semantic context.
* **Research**: Synthesizes market insights.
* **Geo**: Territory visualizer and map builder.
* **Website**: Technical SEO crawler.
* **SEO**: Copywriting and metadata optimizer.
* **Communication**: Adapts multi-channel outreach strategies.
* **Analytics**: Models performance and anomalies.
* **Forecast**: Calculates pipeline LTV and expansion.
* **Document**: Compiles proposals and contracts.
* **Learning**: Analyzes campaign outcomes.
* **Automation**: Executes recurring triggers.

## 7. Memory Engine
Continuous feedback loop containing:
* **Working**: Current context.
* **Episodic**: Interactive sessions.
* **Semantic**: General business rules.
* **Preferences**: Custom user workflows.
* **Business Memory**: Account ledger and historical state.

## 8. Business Model
Unified data graph matching prospects, active clients, active campaigns, communications, metrics, generated documents, and actual outcomes.

## 9. Mission Control
Daily executive briefing answering:
* What deserves attention today?
* What high-yield opportunities were discovered?
* Which clients or leads need immediate follow-up?
* Which campaigns require automated interventions?

## 10. Document Studio
Automates generation of proposals, visual audits, and client reports.

## 11. Security Model
* Secure least-privilege role boundaries.
* Automatic tamper-proof audit logging.
* Cryptographic field-level encryption for personal identifying information (PII).
* Operator confirmations required for sensitive outreach/campaign delivery.

## 12. Roadmap
1. **Foundation**: Structured ledger, secure storage, and basic pipeline.
2. **Intelligence**: Automated local harvesting, geo scoring, reputation scraping. (CURRENT PHASE)
3. **Automation**: Campaign triggers and scheduled outreach.
4. **Multi-agent**: Distributed autonomous optimization.
5. **Expansion**: Global enterprise deployment.
